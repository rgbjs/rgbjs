Check an RGB invoice or file

Paste an invoice or contract ID, or drop a consignment (.rgb) or issuer file. rgbjs works out which RGB line it belongs to, checks it with that line's own code, and shows what it says. Nothing leaves this page unless you choose to look up witness transactions on chain.

Did I get paid? Add the invoice secret you saved

Paste the secret backup from Create (or drop the .secret.json file), then drop the transfer above. rgbjs rebuilds your seal from it and shows what the transfer pays you. The secret stays in this page.

Examples:
Early development, not for real funds. A valid consignment is valid if its witness transactions confirm: rgbjs doesn't look them up on chain, so check them against your own node or a block explorer before you rely on a transfer.
rgbjs runs each RGB line's own Rust code, compiled to WebAssembly: v0.11 (rgb-protocol 0.11.1) and v0.12 (RGB-WG 0.12.0-rc.3). See the conformance tests and the rules.
