- `demo.contract.rgb`: a NIA contract consignment from the pinned `rgb-cmd` 0.11.1, made offline: `rgb -n regtest import NonInflatableAsset.rgb`, `rgb -n regtest issue ssi:rgbjs-test demo.yaml`, `rgb export <id> demo.contract.rgb`. The CLI reported the contract ID `rgb:v4Kpkuwn-yjzKgYa-5G5h0cc-lLVyr2B-hdpCTaR-KOe~agc`. Its genesis seal is the mainnet genesis coinbase output: a test vector, not a real asset.

- `transfer-demo.transfer.rgb`: a transfer consignment made by `rust/gen-v011` (see its `src/main.rs`) with `rgb-api` 0.11.1, without any indexer or network. A NIA contract `rgb:epeKJw9q-4ciD_0S-NChLqoR-5INEwmm-lDL1OEH-ujhEX6Y` is issued on it, and the wallet pays the blinded invoice `rgb:epeKJw9q-…/~/BF/bcrt:utxob:dTwHqt40-IEPM8Yu-nFIXTvY-va2Nh7Y-xXpTu2P-F5DqjgD-0Hh4B?assignment_name=assetOwner` (100 units) with `RgbWallet::pay`, as `rgb transfer` does. The witness `2c54de90…efa5` is unsigned. The pinned `rgb-cmd` 0.11.1 can't validate it offline: its `validate` and `accept` resolve witnesses through an indexer.
